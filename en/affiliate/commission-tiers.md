# Commission Tiers & Upgrades

Your commission rate isn't fixed — it's determined by your **tier (L1–L7)**. The higher your tier, the larger your share of each referral package purchase.

Entry: **Affiliation** → top right **Upgrade commission tier** → `/affiliate/upgrade`

***

## Tiers and rates

| Tier | Name | Commission rate |
|------|------|-----------------|
| Not activated | Basic | 0 |
| L1 | Community Member | 10% |
| L2 | Referral Partner | 20% |
| L3 | Growth Partner | 30% |
| L4 | Channel Partner | 40% |
| L5 | Market Partner | 50% |
| L6 | Strategic Partner | 60% |
| L7 | Ecosystem Partner (highest public tier) | 70% |

> Higher rates require purchasing the corresponding tier separately; the page lists the **upgrade price difference**: new tier price − credit for the tier you've already paid for.

***

## How to upgrade

### L1: activated after one purchase

Buy any **Gas package** or **commission tier**, and the system automatically activates L1 (10%).

### L2–L6: automatic upgrade based on referral count

| Target tier | "Direct referral" requirement |
|-------------|-------------------------------|
| L2 | 3 direct referrals at L1 or above |
| L3 | 5 direct referrals at L1 or above (at least 3 of them at L2) |
| L4 | 8 direct referrals at L1 or above (at least 3 of them at L3) |
| L5 | 12 direct referrals at L1 or above (at least 3 of them at L4) |
| L6 | 20 direct referrals at L1 or above (at least 3 of them at L5) |

Automatic upgrades **require no application** and take effect as soon as you qualify.

### Top tier: must be purchased

L7 is the highest publicly available tier and must be purchased with USDC on the **Upgrade commission tier** page (you only pay the difference when upgrading).

***

## What the upgrade page offers

- Choose a tier to buy and see your commission rate **after upgrading**
- **Earnings simulator**: enter "active referrals" and "monthly package spend per referral" to estimate your monthly commission
- **What you unlock**: higher commission, pay with your USDC balance, takes effect immediately after payment, on-chain settlement to your deposit address
- Confirm the purchase: check the **list price, credit applied, and actual amount to pay** before confirming

***

## Notes

- Commission figures are **only an earnings indication and do not guarantee the amount actually received**
- Tiers depend only on **direct referrals**; indirect referrals don't count
- Tier purchases use the **USDC balance in your custodial account** (the same balance as on the deposit page)
- Promotions and rules may change; go by what the page currently shows
