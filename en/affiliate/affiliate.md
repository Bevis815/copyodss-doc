# Affiliate Program (Affiliation)

Share your invite link with friends — once they sign up and make a purchase, you earn commission.

Entry: **Affiliation** → `/affiliate`

***

## What's on the page

| Section | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Invite link / Invite code** | Copy the link or code and send it to friends; signing up through your link links them to you automatically |
| **Current commission rate** | Determined by your tier; see [Commission Tiers](commission-tiers.md) |
| **Invited users / Estimated earnings** | Summary data |
| **Referral list** | Search by user ID or invite code to see your referrals' tiers and contributions |
| **Telegram bot link** | Shown if the team has assigned you a dedicated bot; otherwise use the web invite link |
| **Auto-upgrade progress** | How many more direct referrals you need for the next tier |

***

## How to earn your first commission

1. Make any purchase (a Gas package or a commission tier) → **L1 is activated automatically**
2. Copy your invite link and send it to friends
3. Your friend signs up through the link
4. Your friend makes a purchase → you earn commission at your current tier's rate

> Commission comes from **your referrals' package purchases**, not from their trading profits; your referrals' trading losses don't affect your commission.

***

## Upgrading your tier

Your tier **upgrades automatically** based on your number of referrals — no application needed. The top tier must be purchased on the **Upgrade commission tier** page. See [Commission Tiers & Upgrades](commission-tiers.md).

***

## FAQ

**My invite code hasn't appeared yet?**  
Generating the invite code takes a moment — refresh the page; if it still doesn't appear, contact support.

**My friend signed up but isn't counted as my referral?**  
Make sure they signed up through your link / invite code; accounts already registered another way can't be linked retroactively.

**When is commission settled?**  
Go by the records shown on the page; settlement depends on your tier rules.

**Will this affect my account security?**  
The affiliate feature never requires you to hand over any password or verification code. **Anyone asking for your verification code, or offering a code-based "unfreeze", is a scammer.**
