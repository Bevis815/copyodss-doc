# What is CopyOdds?

CopyOdds helps you **discover smart money traders on Polymarket and copy them automatically**.

You don't need to watch the market or place orders by hand: when a trader you follow gets a fill on Polymarket, the system tries to mirror the order in your **CopyOdds trading account** according to the rules you set.

## Who it's for

- People who want to copy top-performing Polymarket wallets without trading manually 24/7
- People who want to screen traders by score, win rate, drawdown, copyability, and similar metrics
- People comfortable with the **custodial trading account** model (the platform holds the wallet; you control withdrawals through login and step-up verification)

## What CopyOdds does / doesn't do

| Does | Doesn't |
|------|---------|
| Shows a smart money leaderboard and trader profiles | Guarantee copy-trading profits |
| Automatically copies buys / sells according to your rules | Exactly replicate every trade's price and outcome (there are delays, slippage, and liquidity differences) |
| Provides deposits, withdrawals, Gas, positions, and trade history | Take on the loss risk of prediction markets for you |
| Provides security features such as 2FA, Passkey, and withdrawal step-up verification | Ask for your seed phrase or private key |

## Core concepts (remember these 4)

1. **Smart Money** — A pool of Polymarket trader addresses shown after scoring and screening
2. **Copy rule** — A set of copy parameters (amount, direction, slippage, etc.) for one leader address
3. **Trading account** — USDC and other funds in your custodial wallet, used to place real orders
4. **Platform Gas** — Service-fee credits in your account; **not** on-chain MATIC / BNB
