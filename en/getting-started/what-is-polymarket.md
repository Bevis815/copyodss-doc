# What is Polymarket?

[Polymarket](https://polymarket.com) is a well-known global **prediction market**: users buy and sell shares on the outcomes of real-world events (elections, sports, crypto, economic data, and more). Prices reflect the market's view of how likely each outcome is.

## How it differs from traditional trading

- You trade shares in an event outcome (e.g. Yes / No), not stocks or the underlying asset of a contract
- Prices usually move between $0 and $1 and can be read roughly as the "market-implied probability"
- After an event resolves, shares of the correct outcome can be redeemed according to the market rules
