# How to Choose a Trader

There's no "guaranteed winner". When choosing, put **copyability, risk, activity, and capital size** ahead of "how much they've made historically".

***

## Recommended selection process

### 1. Set your own constraints first

- Max amount per copy trade (or % of balance)
- The maximum drawdown and losing streak you can tolerate
- Preferred market types (politics / sports / crypto, etc.)

### 2. Narrow the pool with filters

- Beginners can start with **Featured**, **High copyability**, **Steady**, and **Low drawdown**
- Avoid styles clearly unsuitable for copying (e.g. heavy market making / extreme gambling patterns) unless you fully understand the risk
- For addresses with very few trades, win rate and returns are of limited value

### 3. Cross-check on the profile page

On the profile page, focus on:

| What to check | Why |
|---------------|-----|
| Trader score + factors | Whether they're suitable to copy overall |
| Drawdown / risk notes | Whether they "win big and draw down big" |
| Last-7-day activity and P&L | Whether they're still trading |
| Copy simulation | Whether they're easy to keep up with under delay (simulation ≠ reality) |
| Real copy performance | More meaningful with enough samples; don't over-read thin samples |

### 4. Test with a small amount

1. Create a rule with a small fixed amount or low percentage
2. Watch the fill / skip / failure reasons in **Trade history** for 1–3 days
3. Then decide whether to increase the amount or switch traders

***

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Safer approach |
|---------|----------------|
| Looking only at the highest total P&L | Also check drawdown, sample size, and copyability |
| Treating simulated ROI as money you'll make | Simulations assume delay/slippage; rely on your own trade history |
| Following anyone with a near-100% win rate | Check whether sample size, open positions, and drawdown add up |
| Copying market-maker "volume" addresses | Regular copying can hardly replicate their fill structure |
| Copying many traders with large amounts at once | Start small and focused; confirm Gas / funds / rules all work |

***

## When to switch traders or reduce size

- Recent activity drops noticeably
- Drawdown or risk notes get worse
- Your trade history shows many slippage failures / long-term skips
- Their style doesn't match your capital size (trades too large or too fragmented)

Once you've picked a trader, see [How to Copy a Trader](../copy-trading/how-to-follow.md).
