# Leaderboard (Copy Pool Daily Profit)

The **Leaderboard** is the App's home page (`/`). It ranks accounts in the platform's copy pool by **daily profit** — essentially a "report card of who's been copying well lately".

The page shows the **board date (business day)**; the business day's start time is as shown on the page.

***

## What's on the page

| Element | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Board · date** | The day this board covers |
| **Daily PnL** | How much the account made on this business day |
| **7D** | P&L over the last 7 days |
| **Current leader / Rank** | Position on the board |
| **View analysis / View account** | Jump to the account's details |
| **Top N / All N** | Show only the top few, or expand the full board |

***

## How to use it

1. Open the App — you land on the Leaderboard by default
2. Look at **Daily PnL + 7D** first; one profitable day doesn't mean consistency
3. Tap **View analysis** or **View account** to see who this account is copying
4. Once you're satisfied, tap **Follow** right on the board to open the copy settings dialog

> Accounts on the board are copiers themselves — their profits show that "copying this kind of trader sometimes works", not that you'll make the same if you copy.

***

## Things to keep in mind

- The board updates daily; yesterday's rank doesn't carry over to today
- Daily profit is easily driven by a single big bet, so **don't just look at #1**
- To pick traders by finer criteria, use the filters in [Smart money](leaderboard.md)
- Board addresses can be shared directly: `https://app.copyodds.io/@0xADDRESS`
